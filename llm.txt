# CrewBridge — Business Summary

CrewBridge is a B2B construction subcontractor recruiting network. We connect pre-vetted subcontractor crews with General Contractors across 10 major US markets.

## What We Do

We match General Contractors with qualified subcontractor crews for residential and light commercial construction projects. Every crew in our network is pre-vetted for licensing, insurance, and work history before they are matched to a GC. This removes the friction of cold-calling subs, checking references, and verifying credentials — GCs get project-ready crews matched to their scope, timeline, and location.

## Business Model

CrewBridge operates on a finder's fee model. General Contractors pay a one-time referral fee when they successfully hire a subcontractor crew through our network. There are no pay-per-lead charges, no monthly subscriptions, and no hidden costs. Subcontractors join the network and receive job referrals at no cost — they negotiate and contract directly with the GC on their own terms.
